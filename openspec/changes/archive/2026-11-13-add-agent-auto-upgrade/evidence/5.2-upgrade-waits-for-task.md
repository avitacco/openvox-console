The task ended at 15:43:21Z (120 s after it started) and the upgrade was handed to dpkg at 15:43:22Z.

How it waited is not how design decision 2 describes it. The console sent `agent_upgrade` at its 15:42:37Z sweep; the client did
not answer "busy". nats.go delivers to a subscription one message at a time and the client runs a task on that delivery
goroutine, so the request sat in the client's queue and was handled when the task returned (`internal/nodeagent/client_test.go`
already documents this delivery behaviour). The outcome is the specified one - the task is never interrupted and the upgrade
follows - but the console's request is outstanding for as long as the task runs, up to its 15 minute timeout.
