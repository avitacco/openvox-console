What this shows:

- **Never downgraded.** au-debian moved 15:40:55Z -> 15:47:41Z -> 16:01:21Z and au-rocky 15:40:55Z -> 16:01:21Z. Once on the
  16:01:21Z client, both stayed there for the two further sweeps observed, although instance X serves an older client and
  both nodes are visible to it; X reports them as current.
- **One instance at a time.** au-rocky was instructed once, by Y. au-debian was instructed by X and then, after it had
  restarted on X's build and reported it, by Y - two upgrades in sequence, four seconds apart, never two outstanding at
  once. Each node's journal shows exactly the upgrades the two console logs account for.
- A node attached to the instance with the older client still ends on the newest client in the cluster, at the cost of one
  intermediate upgrade when the older instance wins the first claim.
